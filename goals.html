A Little Reminder to Myself

Hi, I'm Milktea! Sometimes a new challenge feels taller than I am. But just like the cat in this picture, I can keep trying.

In my imaginary cat school, I want to learn new things, make happy memories, and become a good friends to my classmates.

I do not have to reach every dream today. I just have to take my next little step.

My promise: try, practice, rest, and try again.

01 — My School Goals

My first goal is to become a curious learner. I want to listen carefully, practice new skills, and finish the activities I start. When something is difficult, I will ask for help and give it another try.

  • Learn one new thing each day.
  • Practice instead of giving up after one mistake.
  • Be kind to my classmates, Pearly and Bonsai.

My first step: Spend a little time practicing before taking my next nap.

02 — My Dream Career

I dream of becoming the Chief Comfort Officer of a cozy home. My job would be to keep my human company, welcome my cat friends, and remind everyone to take a break. I want to make every ordinary day feel a little warmer.

  • Offer excellent nap-time company.
  • Check every cardboard box for comfort.
  • Bring smiles to my human and friends.

My first step: Practice being a patient, gentle companion.

03 — My Future Plans

In the future, I hope to have a sunny window, a soft bed, and plenty of time with the ones I love. I want to fill my scrapbook with new memories. Most of all, I want to keep growing into a happy and brave little cat.

  1. Explore a new cozy corner at home.
