Learning Adventures

ENTRY 01 | LIFE WITH MOM

I'm always her baby

As the eldest of the bunch, I'm the boss and what I meow will always be right. They can't do anything about it or I will slap them with my claw.

But with my mother, I'm always a baby, a single pat of my mother through her chest signals me to lay down on her and purr my heart out.

Being with my Mom calms me and, ofcourse, it's the same for her, every purr and knead heals both our hearts

I believe: No matter how bad my attitude went, my moom will always treat me as her precious baby.

ENTRY 02 | LIFE WITH MY CAT FRIENDS

A Quiet Afternoon

Some of my favorite days are the quiet ones. This afternoon, the gang, and I found a comfortable place to rest. There was no big adventure, just good company and a peaceful moment at home.

My Mom took photos of us. Each picture reminds me of a different little moment. I like how an ordinary afternoon can become a memory worth keeping.

That is why I made a gallery for my website. I want a place for the people, friends, and moments that make me happy. When I look at these photos, I remember that a good story does not always need a big event.

What I learned: The simplest moments can be the ones we treasure most.
